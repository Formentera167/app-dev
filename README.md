# My Favorite Anime: Solo Leveling

> "I will protect my family, even if it means all the hunters in the world become my enemies." – Sung Jin-woo

## Overview
**Solo Leveling** is an action/dark fantasy series set in a world where portals connecting our world to dungeons opened up. It follows *Sung Jin-woo*, known as the weakest hunter of all mankind, who gains a unique system allowing him to level up infinitely.

### Main Characters
1. **Sung Jin-woo** - *Shadow Monarch & E-Rank turned S-Rank Hunter*
2. **Cha Hae-in** - *S-Rank Hunter & Swordmaster*
3. **Yoo Jin-ho** - *D-Rank Hunter & Vice-Chairman of Ahjin Guild*
4. **Go Gun-hee** - *Chairman of the Korean Hunters Association*

### Key Features of the Series
* **Insane Power Progression:** Watching Jin-woo transform from weak to unstoppable.
* **Shadow Army:** The epic ability to extract and command defeated monsters.
* **Top-Tier Production:** Outstanding animation and soundtrack by A-1 Pictures and Hiroyuki Sawano.

## Why I Like It
* Incredible fight choreography and visual effects
* Highly satisfying power fantasy and leveling system
* Hypnotic soundtrack during boss fights
* Epic army battles with memorable shadow summons

## Series Information
* **Genre:** Action / Dark Fantasy / System
* **Release Year:** 2024
* **Studio:** A-1 Pictures
